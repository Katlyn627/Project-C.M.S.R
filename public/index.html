🌍 Mission & Safeguarding Architecture

Project C.M.S.R is designed to protect vulnerable adolescent students (particularly young girls) commuting to secondary schools and after-school youth centers across low-connectivity regions. The platform coordinates walking-bus escorts, enforces safety ratios, and securely handles incident escalations.

🔒 Zero-Plaintext Child Data

All incident descriptions and involved-party identifiers are encrypted at rest using authenticated AES-256-GCM cryptography. Only verified coordinators and admins possess role-based decryption clearance.

📡 Low-Bandwidth SMS & USSD

Designed for field environments with minimal cellular data. Volunteers receive automated shift notifications and can confirm attendance or report issues via simulated USSD callback sessions.
